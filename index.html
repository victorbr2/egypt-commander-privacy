Privacy Policy

Information about how the game handles player data and online services.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction and data controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Egypt Commander (the “Game”), developed by Victor Bosch (“we”, “us” or “the developer”), handles information when you play the Game.

For privacy questions or requests, contact: vbr084299@gmail.com.

2. Information processed by the Game

The Game uses Unity Authentication with anonymous sign-in. Players do not need to provide an email address or password to use this sign-in method.

When online authentication and leaderboard features are used, the following information is processed:

  • Unity Player ID: a unique identifier generated by Unity Authentication to identify a player in Unity Gaming Services.
  • IP address: processed by Unity Leaderboards as part of providing the leaderboard service.
  • Player display name: a name chosen by the player in the Game and shown in online rankings. Players should use a nickname and avoid entering their real name or other identifying information.
  • Leaderboard scores: scores submitted by the Game and associated with the player's leaderboard entry.

We do not ask players to submit an email address or password for anonymous authentication.

3. Why this information is processed

The information described above is used to:

  • create and recognise an anonymous player identity;
  • submit, store and display scores in online leaderboards;
  • show player rankings and display names; and
  • operate, maintain and protect the Game's online features.

4. Third-party service provider: Unity

The Game uses the following Unity Gaming Services:

  • Unity Authentication for anonymous player identification;
  • Unity Leaderboards to store and display player scores and rankings.

Unity Technologies processes information to provide these services. Under applicable European data protection law, Unity describes itself as a processor for these services and the game developer as the controller. You can read more in Unity's Authentication privacy overview, Leaderboards privacy overview and Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Leaderboard entries, including display names and scores, may be visible to other players who access the rankings.

5. Local game data

Gameplay progress and game settings are saved locally on the player's device. This local data may be lost if the Game is uninstalled, application data is cleared, or the device is reset. Local save data is separate from online leaderboard entries.

6. Data retention

Local game data remains on the device until it is deleted by the player or removed through device or application settings.

Unity's Leaderboards documentation states that leaderboard personal data is retained indefinitely by default, or until the relevant leaderboard is reset. If leaderboard archives are enabled, archived data may also be retained indefinitely. The applicable retention behaviour depends on the service configuration.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell players' personal information. Information is processed by Unity as necessary to provide the online authentication and leaderboard features. We rely on the service provider's technical and organisational security measures for the hosted services and take reasonable steps in operating the Game.

8. Privacy rights and requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

To submit a privacy request concerning Egypt Commander, email vbr084299@gmail.com and include “Egypt Commander privacy request” in the subject. We may need information sufficient to identify the relevant player record. We will assess and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law. Some requests involving Unity-hosted leaderboard data may require action through Unity's service tools.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is not designed to request children's personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through a player display name or another feature, please contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its services or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
